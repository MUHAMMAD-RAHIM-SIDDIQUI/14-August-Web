National Symbols of Pakistan

Our Pride • Our Identity

National Animal

The Markhor, a wild goat species, symbolizes strength and resilience.

National Bird

The Chukar Partridge represents freedom, beauty, and tradition of Pakistan.

National Flower

The Jasmine flower symbolizes purity, simplicity, and peace.

National Fruit

The Mango, king of fruits, represents sweetness, summer, and hospitality.

National Sport

Field Hockey is Pakistan’s national sport, bringing global pride and medals.

National Dress

The Shalwar Kameez is the cultural identity and traditional attire of Pakistan.
